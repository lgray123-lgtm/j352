Justin Bieber Throughout The Years


Justin Bieber started off as a normal kid in Stratford, Ontario, Canada. He learned instruments like the guitar at a young age and would busk on the sidewalk steps outside of a theater in his hometown at the age of 12. His mother began posting videos of him on YouTube which eventually grabbed the attention of Scooter Braun, who was a record executive at the time. Ever since then, his fame has continued to grow and he is now one of the biggest artists of all time.
